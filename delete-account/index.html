Haven 52 Get the app

Delete your account

You can delete your Haven 52 account at any time. Deleting is permanent: there is no grace period and no undo.

In the app

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Press Delete account, then confirm with Delete account again.

The account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Write to the developer email address shown on Haven 52's Google Play listing with the subject “Delete my account”. Include your player ID, which other players also see on your profile, and, for a linked account, send it from the Google account you signed in with so the request can be matched to you.

A guest account that was never linked lives only on its device. If the app has been removed or its data cleared, that account is already gone and nobody can bring it back.

What deletion removes

  • Your profile: display name, avatar and player ID. The ID can no longer be found or signed in to, and your name is scrubbed.
  • Your coins and your progression: EXP and level.
  • Your friends, friend requests, blocks and push tokens.
  • The link to your Google account or device, so the same login starts a fresh account next time.

What stays

Coin and match rows stay without anything personal in them, so other players' own history stays whole.

More detail is in the privacy policy.
